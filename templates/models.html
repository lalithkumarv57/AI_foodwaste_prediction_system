Scientific Methodology & Reliability Safeguards
No Lookahead Leakage

Lag features (Lag_1, Lag_7, Rolling_Mean_7) are computed strictly with a shift(1) lag, ensuring today's sales are never accessible when making today's forecast.

Chronological Split

Standard random cross-validation leaks time-series dependencies. Our chronological validation tests the model on unseen future dates.

Non-Negative Bound

The model output is constrained by MAX(0, prediction) to prevent nonsensical negative food demand predictions.
