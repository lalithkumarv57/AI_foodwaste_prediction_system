AI-Driven Food Waste Mitigation Framework
1. Batch Downsizing

For items with high perishability (e.g. Steamed Rice & Salads), schedule two mid-day micro-batches instead of a single giant morning batch.

2. Dynamic Buffers

Replace fixed 20% safety rules with ML standard deviation bands that auto-tighten during stable mid-week days.

3. Weather Adaptations

Curries and soups gain 15-20% demand spikes during rain/cold; salads drop by 18%. Automatic ML feature inputs adjust prep accordingly.

4. Leftover Repurposing

If usable inventory remains within safe HACCP food-holding limits, deduct directly from next day's batch recommendation.
